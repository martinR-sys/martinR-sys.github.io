The rule, in plain words

  • 5% down. Owner-occupant on a 2- to 4-unit building. Before November 2023 it was 15% on a duplex and 25% on a 3- or 4-unit building.
  • 75% of the rent counts. The lender counts 75% of the market rent on the units you do not live in. The other 25% is held for vacancies and upkeep.
  • 6 months of reserves. After closing, 6 months of the full monthly payment must be in the bank. Gift money cannot cover reserves.
  • PMI below 20% down. Required by the lender and removed once the loan balance reaches 80% of the home’s value.
  • Move in within 60 days. 2026 conforming limit on a 4-unit building: $1,601,750.
